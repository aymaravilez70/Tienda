# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, Three.js / React Three Fiber, and Playwright, as selected and installed for this project.

## Users

- The shop owner, who maintains the inventory and uploads or replaces product photos.
- The shop's customers, who browse products and contact the owner directly to ask about or order an item.

## Product Purpose

Present the shop's product range in a digital catalog that the owner can keep up to date as stock changes. Success means customers can find products and start a direct WhatsApp conversation with the shop.

## Positioning

An editable catalog for a small multi-category shop, with product inquiries handled directly through WhatsApp rather than an online checkout.

## Operating Context

The business sells fashion and novelty products. The owner expects to maintain roughly 30 to 50 different products and supplies the product photography.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products cover calzado, ropa, accesorios, and bisutería.
- The owner needs to add, edit, hide, or remove products and update photos and stock availability.
- Customers should contact the shop through WhatsApp. The shop's complete WhatsApp number has not been provided yet.
- No cart or online payment flow is currently in scope.
- The business name is not confirmed. “Novedades” is approved only as temporary header text.
- Domain, hosting, data configuration, and the exact catalog management flow remain to be arranged.

## Evidence on Hand

The project brief and customer conversation are in the chat. Product photos and a confirmed business logo/name have not been provided.

## Product Principles

- Keep products easy for the owner to maintain.
- Make browsing and stock availability clear to customers.
- Make direct contact easy without introducing checkout steps.
